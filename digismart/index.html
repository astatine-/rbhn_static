Password Security - Basics

🔐 Password Categories:

  • HIGH-RISK: Banking, investment → 16+ chars, unique
  • MEDIUM-RISK: Email, social media → 12+ chars
  • LOW-RISK: Streaming, news → Simpler OK

❌ NEVER Use:

  • password123, name+year, birthday, phone number
  • Same password everywhere

✅ Password Manager - Bitwarden:

  • FREE, open-source
  • Generates unique passwords for each account
  • You remember ONE master password
  • Available: bitwarden.com
Not all accounts need same security. High-risk financial needs 16+ chars, unique. Use Bitwarden - free, generates strong passwords, you only remember one master password.

2FA & Passkeys - Essential Protection

✅ Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) - CRITICAL:

  • Enable on ALL financial accounts
  • Even if password stolen, 2FA protects you
  • Prevents 99% of account takeovers

Types: SMS OTP (good) | Authenticator app (better) | Hardware key (best)

🔑 Passkeys (The Future - NEW!):

  • Uses fingerprint/face instead of password
  • Cannot be phished or stolen
  • Supported by Google, Apple, some banks
  • Check: Settings → Passkey/Passwordless

🎯 ACTION: Enable 2FA on your bank account TODAY

2FA is CRITICAL. Even weak password + 2FA = safe. 99% of takeovers prevented. Passkeys are future - fingerprint instead of password, impossible to phish. Enable 2FA on everything financial TODAY.

Phishing - Email Scams

📧 Example: Fake Bank Email

From: noreply@hdfc-bank.co.in (fake!)

Subject: "Account will be closed"

Body: "Click here to verify within 24 hours..."

🚩 Red Flags:

  • Fake domain (real: hdfcbank.bank.in)
  • Generic greeting ("Dear Customer")
  • Urgent language
  • Asks you to click link

✅ Protection:

  • NEVER click links in emails
  • Open banking app directly
  • Real banks use .bank.in domains
  • Call bank using number on YOUR card
Phishing is foundation of most scams. Email looks real but domain reveals truth: hdfc-bank.co.in vs hdfcbank.bank.in. Creates fear and urgency. Never click links - open app or type URL yourself.

Phishing - SMS Scams

📱 Common SMS Scams:

  • Fake UPI: "Payment of ₹25,000 failed. Click to retry"
    🚩 Shortened URL, creates panic
  • Account Lock: "Account locked. Verify now: [link]"
    🚩 Sent from mobile number (banks use 6-char IDs)
  • Prize/Lottery: "Won ₹50,000. Claim here"
    🚩 You didn't enter any lottery

✅ How to Verify:

  • Real banks SMS from 6-character IDs ("HDFC", "ICICI")
  • NOT from 10-digit mobile numbers
  • Never click links in SMS
  • Check app directly if concerned
SMS phishing very common. Creates panic with large amounts. Real banks use registered 6-character sender IDs since 2024 (TRAI regulation). Mobile number = scam. Never click SMS links.

WhatsApp Scams - Part 1

🟢 Hacked Contact Scam:

Friend's WhatsApp hacked → "Emergency, send ₹10,000 urgently"

Protection: ALWAYS call person directly (phone call, not WhatsApp)

🟢 Investment Groups:

"Stock Tips - 100% Returns" group → 50-100 fake members → Fake success stories → Invest ₹1L → App disappears

Protection: Exit unsolicited groups | Check SEBI.gov.in before investing

✅ Family Codeword: Establish secret word for money requests. If they don't say it = scam

WhatsApp scams feel personal. Hacked contacts: emergency money request from friend's number - call them directly. Investment groups: ALL fake accounts performing for you. Small profit is bait. Check SEBI always.

WhatsApp Scams - Part 2

🟢 APK Files - EXTREMELY DANGEROUS:

"Download app for cashback" → Install APK file → Spyware reads passwords, captures OTPs, steals contacts

Protection: NEVER install APK files from WhatsApp | Only use Play Store/App Store

📞 Call Forwarding:

Scammer: "Dial *401*[number]#" → Forwards ALL your calls/OTPs to scammer

Protection: NEVER dial *codes* | Check: *#21# | Disable: ##21#

Android Users:

Settings → Security → Unknown Sources → DISABLE

APK files are malware - install spyware that captures everything. Only install from official stores. Call forwarding: *401* forwards calls to scammer - they get your OTPs. Never dial codes someone tells you.

Screen Sharing Scams

💻 How It Works:

  1. "Install AnyDesk to process refund"
  2. You install (legitimate app)
  3. You give access code
  4. They SEE and CONTROL your phone
  5. Watch you enter passwords, transfer money

Real Example (Delhi 2024):

₹2,50,000 lost in minutes while watching helplessly

🛑 THE TRUTH:

  • Banks NEVER ask for screen sharing
  • E-commerce NEVER needs remote access
  • Delivery companies DON'T use these apps

Anyone asks for AnyDesk/TeamViewer = HANG UP

AnyDesk/TeamViewer are legitimate IT tools but scammers abuse. They watch you log in, see passwords, control phone. Real case: ₹2.5 lakhs gone in minutes. No legitimate company asks for this. Hang up immediately.

Fake Websites - Perfect Forgeries

🌐 Real vs Fake:

  • ✓ hdfcbank.bank.in = REAL
  • ❌ hdfc-bank.com = FAKE
  • ❌ hdfcbank-secure.net = FAKE
  • ❌ hdfcbank.co.in = FAKE

✅ How to Spot Fake Sites:

  • Check URL: Real banks end in .bank.in
  • 🚩 Extra hyphens (hdfc-bank)
  • 🚩 Wrong extension (.com instead of .bank.in)
  • 🚩 Misspellings (hdfc-bnk.com)

✅ Protection:

  • Type URL yourself (don't click links)
  • Use banking apps (safer than websites)
  • Check URL BEFORE entering password
  • Government sites: .gov.in only
Fake sites look IDENTICAL to real ones. Only URL reveals truth. .bank.in since 2024 - scammers can't register. Check character by character before entering password. Appearance can be faked - URL is only truth.

OTP Trap - #1 Scam in India

How It Works:

  1. "HDFC security. Suspicious activity detected"
  2. "Someone tried to withdraw ₹50,000"
  3. "Sending OTP. Read it to verify it's you"
  4. OTP arrives (REAL, from your bank)
  5. You share: "1-2-3-4-5-6"
  6. Scammer uses it → Money GONE

🛑 THE ABSOLUTE TRUTH:

Banks will NEVER ask for OTP. Not on phone, not via email, NEVER EVER.

Safe to Share OTPs:

✓ Food delivery | ✓ Package delivery | ❌ NEVER financial OTPs

OTP scam is #1 in India. Psychology: Authority, Fear, Urgency. OTP is REAL from bank but triggered by scammer. By giving it, you authorize them. Bangalore case: 3 OTPs, ₹15 lakhs lost. Some OTPs safe (delivery). Financial: NEVER.

Courier / Digital Arrest Scam

📦 How It Works:

Stage 1: Digital arrest call → Transfer ₹2,00,000

Stage 2: "Courier will collect documents"

Next day: Real courier arrives → Collects Aadhaar, PAN, passport + ₹75,000 cash

Result: ₹2,75,000 lost + identity theft

✅ Protection:

  • Government doesn't arrest via courier
  • Never give original documents to couriers
  • If threatened with arrest → Visit police station yourself
Courier scam is digital arrest escalation. After ₹2 lakhs digital transfer, send courier for documents and cash. Documents used for identity theft. Government doesn't work this way. Go to police station yourself if threatened.

UPI Mistaken Transfer Scam

💸 How It Works:

  1. Notification: "Received ₹5,000"
  2. WhatsApp: "Sorry, wrong number. Return urgently"
  3. You feel obligated, send ₹5,000 back
  4. Hours later: Original ₹5,000 FAILS (was from stolen account)
  5. Your return was REAL → You lost ₹5,000

✅ Protection:

  • NEVER return unexpected money immediately
  • Wait 24-48 hours for settlement
  • Check actual bank balance (not just notification)
  • Let bank handle reversal through official process

🎯 QR Code Safety:

Generate YOUR OWN QR to receive | Never scan buyer's QR

UPI reversal exploits honesty. Payment shows received but hasn't settled. You return money that was never real, but your return is real. Wait 24-48 hours always. QR: generate your own to receive money.

Digital Arrest - Exploiting Fear

🚨 Setup:

"CBI. You're involved in money laundering. Arrest warrant issued."

Stages:

  1. Call creating fear: "Arrest in 24 hours"
  2. Video call: Fake police uniform, fake documents
  3. App download: "Digital Arrest Portal" = spyware
  4. Money extraction: "Transfer to govt account" ₹2-5 lakhs

🛑 THE TRUTH:

  • ❌ Police DON'T arrest via phone/video
  • ❌ No "digital arrest" app exists
  • ❌ Government DOESN'T ask for money
  • ✓ Real police come with warrant

If you get such call: Hang up → Go to police station → File complaint

Digital arrest fastest growing scam 2024. Exploits fear, authority, shame. Sophisticated: video calls with uniforms. Psychology: isolation, urgency. Delhi woman ₹2.5 crore. Government doesn't arrest by phone. Period. Go to police station yourself.

3 Rules = 90% Protection

RULE 1: DON'T CLICK LINKS

❌ Bank emails, tax refunds, account updates

✓ Type URL yourself or use banking app

Why: 80% of scams start with clicked link

RULE 2: DON'T ACT ON MESSAGES

❌ Friend asking for money, account locked alerts

✓ Call person/company directly, use official app

✓ Exception: Transaction alerts OK to read (don't click)

RULE 3: DON'T INSTALL UNKNOWN APPS

❌ APK files from WhatsApp, apps from websites

✓ Only: Play Store (Android) or App Store (iPhone)

✓ Android: Disable "Unknown Sources" in Settings

Three simple rules prevent 90% of scams. Never click links - even if they look real. Don't act on messages alone - verify separately. Only install from official stores. APK files = malware. When in doubt, STOP and verify.

Advanced Protection

🏦 Separate Bank Accounts:

Main (₹5L) - minimal activity | Secondary (₹20K) - UPI/shopping

Benefit: If compromised, only ₹20K at risk

⚙️ Set Transaction Limits:

UPI limit: ₹5-10K (not ₹1L) | Card: ₹10-20K

How: Banking app → Settings → Limits

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Secret Phrase:

Agree on codeword (e.g., "Rainbow42")

Rule: Any money request MUST include codeword

Defeats: Hacked WhatsApp, SMS spoofing, AI voice cloning

📊 Monthly Checks:

Review bank statements | Enable transaction alerts | Check credit report

Advanced strategies: Separate accounts compartmentalizes damage. Limits restrict loss. Family codeword defeats ALL impersonation including AI. Monthly checks catch unauthorized charges early. Setup takes time but provides powerful protection.

AI Deepfakes - The New Threat

🤖 What Are Deepfakes?

AI-generated fake video/audio that looks/sounds 100% real

Only 30 seconds of voice needed to clone perfectly

💔 Real Indian Cases (2024):

  • Mumbai Mother (₹80K): Call from "son" - voice EXACTLY matched. "Arrested in Dubai, need bail." Son was safe at home.
  • Delhi Officer (₹74K): VIDEO call showing "niece" - perfect match. Real niece at work, knew nothing.
  • Celebrity Deepfakes: Fake Mukesh Ambani, Virat Kohli promoting scam apps

⚠️ The Reality:

Video/voice can NO LONGER be trusted alone | 83% of Indians affected by AI voice scams (2023)

Deepfakes are newest threat. AI creates perfect fake video/audio. Mumbai mother heard son's EXACT voice. Delhi officer SAW niece on video. Both fake. Technology here, improving fast. 83% Indians affected 2023. Cannot trust video/audio alone anymore.

Deepfake Protection

🚩 How to Spot:

Video: Lip sync issues | Unnatural blinking | Hair edges blurry | Weird shadows

Audio: Unusual pauses | Robotic tone | Inconsistent quality

Behavioral: Out of character | Urgency | Can't verify | Avoiding questions

🛡️ Protection:

  1. STOP - Don't send money
  2. CALL person using number YOU know
  3. ASK personal questions only they'd know
  4. USE family codeword (defeats ALL deepfakes!)
  5. CHECK detection tools: deepware.ai (video), resemble.ai (audio)

🔑 Family codeword is now ESSENTIAL, not optional

Spotting deepfakes getting harder but still possible. Family codeword defeats this - AI can clone voice/face but can't know secret word. Free detection tools work. Video/voice no longer proof of identity. Verification through independent channel ESSENTIAL.

If You Suspect Fraud

⚡ Immediate Actions:

  1. Login to bank app (NOT via link) - check balance/transactions
  2. Disable/reduce limits - Block card OR reduce to ₹100
  3. Call bank - Number on YOUR card (not from email/Google)

📞 Timing = Recovery:

  • Within 1 hour: Card 80-90% | UPI 20-30%
  • After 24 hours: Card 70% | UPI 5-10%
  • After 1 week: <5% (money gone)

📝 If Money Gone:

  • Within 1 hr: Call bank, report fraud
  • Within 24 hrs: cybercrime.gov.in complaint
  • Within 48 hrs: Police FIR (needed for claims)
Every minute counts. Login directly, check transactions. Reduce limits to stop further damage. Call bank immediately. Within 1 hour, card fraud 80-90% recovery. After 24 hours drops dramatically. If money gone: bank, cybercrime.gov.in, police FIR. Speed is everything.
