🔐 Password Security - Basics

Password Categories

Three levels of password security:

  • HIGH-RISK: Banking, investment → 16+ characters, unique
  • MEDIUM-RISK: Email, social media → 12+ characters
  • LOW-RISK: Streaming, news → Simpler is okay

📧 Phishing - Email Scams

Example: Fake Bank Email

From: noreply@hdfc-bank.co.in (fake!)

Subject: "Account will be closed"

Body: "Click here to verify within 24 hours..."

🚩 Red Flags:

  • Fake domain (real: hdfcbank.bank.in)
  • Generic greeting ("Dear Customer")
  • Urgent language
  • Asks you to click link

Protection

✅ How to Stay Safe:

  • NEVER click links in emails
  • Open banking app directly
  • Real banks use .bank.in domains
  • Call bank using number on YOUR card

📱 Phishing - SMS Scams

Common SMS Scams

Fake UPI: "Payment of ₹25,000 failed. Click to retry"

🚩 Shortened URL, creates panic

Account Lock: "Account locked. Verify now: [link]"

🚩 Sent from mobile number (banks use 6-char IDs)

Prize/Lottery: "Won ₹50,000. Claim here"

🚩 You didn't enter any lottery

How to Verify

✅ Real vs Fake:

  • Real banks SMS from 6-character IDs ("HDFC", "ICICI")
  • NOT from 10-digit mobile numbers
  • Never click links in SMS
  • Check app directly if concerned

🟢 WhatsApp Scams - Part 1

Hacked Contact Scam

How it works:

Friend's WhatsApp hacked → "Emergency, send ₹10,000 urgently"

Protection: ALWAYS call person directly (phone call, not WhatsApp)

Investment Groups

The Setup:

"Stock Tips - 100% Returns" group → 50-100 fake members → Fake success stories → Invest ₹1L → App disappears

Protection:

  • Exit unsolicited groups immediately
  • Check SEBI.gov.in before investing
  • All group members are fake accounts

✅ Family Codeword: Establish secret word for money requests. If they don't say it = scam

🟢 WhatsApp Scams - Part 2

APK Files - EXTREMELY DANGEROUS

How it works:

"Download app for cashback" → Install APK file → Spyware reads passwords, captures OTPs, steals contacts

Protection:

  • NEVER install APK files from WhatsApp
  • Only use Play Store/App Store
  • Android: Settings → Security → Unknown Sources → DISABLE

Call Forwarding Trap

The Scam:

Scammer: "Dial 401[number]#" → Forwards ALL your calls/OTPs to scammer

Protection:

  • NEVER dial codes someone tells you
  • Check if forwarding is active: *#21#
  • Disable any forwarding: ##21#

How It Works

The Process:

1. "Install AnyDesk to process refund"

4. They SEE and CONTROL your phone

5. Watch you enter passwords, transfer money

Real Example (Delhi 2024): ₹2,50,000 lost in minutes while watching helplessly

The Truth

🛑 REALITY CHECK:

  • Banks NEVER ask for screen sharing
  • E-commerce NEVER needs remote access
  • Delivery companies DON'T use these apps

Anyone asks for AnyDesk/TeamViewer = HANG UP

🌐 Fake Websites - Perfect Forgeries

Real vs Fake

Examples:

  • ✓ hdfcbank.bank.in = REAL
  • ❌ hdfc-bank.com = FAKE
  • ❌ hdfcbank-secure.net = FAKE
  • ❌ hdfcbank.co.in = FAKE

How to Spot Fake Sites

✅ Red Flags to Check:

  • Check URL BEFORE entering password
  • 🚩 Extra hyphens (hdfc-bank)
  • 🚩 Wrong extension (.com instead of .bank.in)
  • 🚩 Misspellings (hdfc-bnk.com)

Protection:

  • Type URL yourself (don't click links)
  • Use banking apps (safer than websites)
  • Real bank domains end in .bank.in
  • Government sites: .gov.in only

🔢 OTP Trap - #1 Scam in India

How It Works

The Process:

1. "HDFC security. Suspicious activity detected"

2. "Someone tried to withdraw ₹50,000"

3. "Sending OTP. Read it to verify it's you"

4. OTP arrives (REAL, from your bank)

5. You share: "1-2-3-4-5-6"

6. Scammer uses it → Money GONE

The Absolute Truth

🛑 CRITICAL:

Banks will NEVER ask for OTP. Not on phone, not via email, NEVER EVER.

Safe to Share OTPs:

✓ Food delivery

✓ Package delivery

❌ NEVER financial OTPs

📦 Courier / Digital Arrest Scam

How It Works

The Setup:

  • Stage 1: Digital arrest call → Transfer ₹2,00,000
  • Stage 2: "Courier will collect documents"
  • Next day: Real courier arrives → Collects Aadhaar, PAN, passport + ₹75,000 cash
  • Result: ₹2,75,000 lost + identity theft

Protection

✅ Reality Check:

  • Government doesn't arrest via courier
  • Never give original documents to couriers
  • If threatened with arrest → Visit police station yourself
  • No legitimate government agency works this way

💸 UPI Mistaken Transfer Scam

How It Works

The Process:

1. Notification: "Received ₹5,000"

2. WhatsApp: "Sorry, wrong number. Return urgently"

3. You feel obligated, send ₹5,000 back

4. Hours later: Original ₹5,000 FAILS (was from stolen account)

5. Your return was REAL → You lost ₹5,000

Protection

✅ How to Stay Safe:

  • NEVER return unexpected money immediately
  • Wait 24-48 hours for settlement
  • Check actual bank balance (not just notification)
  • Let bank handle reversal through official process

🎯 QR Code Safety: Generate YOUR OWN QR to receive | Never scan buyer's QR

🤖 AI Deepfakes - The New Threat

What Are Deepfakes?

AI-generated fake video/audio that looks/sounds 100% real

Only 30 seconds of voice needed to clone perfectly

Real Indian Cases (2024)

Mumbai Mother (₹80K):

Call from "son" - voice EXACTLY matched. "Arrested in Dubai, need bail." Son was safe at home.

Delhi Officer (₹74K):

VIDEO call showing "niece" - perfect match. Real niece at work, knew nothing.

Celebrity Deepfakes:

Fake Mukesh Ambani, Virat Kohli promoting scam apps

⚠️ The Reality: Video/voice can NO LONGER be trusted alone | 83% of Indians affected by AI voice scams (2023)

🛡️ Deepfake Protection

How to Spot

🚩 Video Signs:

Lip sync issues | Unnatural blinking | Hair edges blurry | Weird shadows

🚩 Audio Signs:

Unusual pauses | Robotic tone | Inconsistent quality

🚩 Behavioral Signs:

Out of character | Urgency | Can't verify | Avoiding questions

Protection Steps

5-Step Defense:

1. STOP - Don't send money

2. CALL person using number YOU know

3. ASK personal questions only they'd know

4. USE family codeword (defeats ALL deepfakes!)

5. CHECK detection tools: deepware.ai (video), resemble.ai (audio)
